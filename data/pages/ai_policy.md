AI assistance is permitted in Rocq and its satellite projects. Contributors and maintainers remain responsible for their work:

- **Understand what you submit.** You must review, understand, and be able to explain every contribution, including AI-assisted code, proofs, documentation, and reviews. We want to keep the project in the hands of humans.
- **Respect reviewers’ time.** Submit focused, tested changes with concise explanations. Avoid bulk-generated PRs and walls of generated text. Maintainers may decline contributions whose review cost outweighs their value.
- **Be transparent.** Disclose how you used an AI, not which agent or model you used.
- **Respect legal obligations.** Do due diligence to ensure that your AI-generated code is not reproducing / directly derived from an existing code (or that its licensing terms are respected) and that you are allowed to include it in the Rocq project under its own licensing terms. Do not submit generated material whose origin or right to reuse is uncertain.
- **Consider environmental costs.** Use AI deliberately and proportionately. Increased output is not a goal in itself; prioritize useful, maintainable work over volume.

Satellite projects may adapt these guidelines to their maintenance capacity and technical needs.

**Human understanding, accountability, and sustainable development practices remain essential.**
